# 📖 Using SummarizeAI

Getting the most out of the platform involves a bit more than just pasting text. Here is how to configure the engine to fit your specific document.

## 🕹 The Workflow

### 1. Document Input
You can either paste your text directly into the main editor or use the **Upload PDF** button at the bottom to handle up to 5 files at once. Note that the engine performs best with inputs between 50 and 15,000 words.

### 2. Tuning the Engine
Under **Advanced Settings**, you'll find the core controls:
* **Method:** Choose **Extractive** if you want the most important original sentences pulled out. Choose **Abstractive** if you want the system to rewrite the ideas in a more conversational way.
* **Target Length:** Use the slider to find your "sweet spot." **Tiny** is great for a quick gist, while **Detailed** keeps more of the nuance.
* **Domain:** Don't skip this. Setting the domain to **Academic**, **Legal**, or **Technical** helps the model prioritize the right terminology.

### 3. Reading the Metrics
Once you hit **Generate Summary**, the right panel populates with your results. Keep an eye on the **Compression Ratio** and **Time Saved** cards—these give you a raw look at how much fluff was cut out.

### 4. History & Favorites
All your runs are stored in the **History** tab. If you find a particularly good summary, use the **Favorite** toggle to pin it so it’s easy to find later.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips
* **Complex Papers:** Set the domain to **Academic** and use **Abstractive** mode for a summary that actually flows well.
* **Quick Skimming:** Use **Extractive** mode with the **Tiny** length setting to find the "bottom line" of a document in seconds.
