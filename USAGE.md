# 📖 Usage Guide: SummarizeAI

Maximize your productivity with **SummarizeAI**. This guide explains how to use our NLP toolkit to get the best results from your documents.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Input Your Content
* **Paste Text:** Paste your text directly into the "Document Content" area.
* **Upload PDF:** Drag and drop up to 5 PDFs into the upload zone at the bottom of the dashboard.
* **Minimum/Maximum:** Ensure your text is between 50 and 15,000 words for optimal analysis.

### 2. Configure Summarization Settings
Before clicking generate, adjust your settings in the **Advanced Settings** toggle:
* **Summarization Method:**
    * **Extractive:** Identifies and preserves the most statistically significant sentences using TF-IDF scoring.
    * **Abstractive:** Rephrases and restructures key ideas into a cohesive, natural narrative.
* **Target Length Slider:**
    * **Tiny:** A high-level overview.
    * **Balanced:** A standard summary of key points.
    * **Detailed:** A deep dive into the content.
* **Domain Awareness:** Select a domain (Academic, Legal, Medical, etc.) to help the engine understand technical terminology.

### 3. Analyze the Results
Once generated, the right panel will display:
* **AI Summary:** The processed text based on your chosen method.
* **Compression Analytics:** Real-time metrics including **Compression Ratio**, **Time Saved**, and **Key Points** extracted.

### 4. Manage Your History
* Every summary is automatically saved to your **History**.
* Use the **History** tab in the navigation bar to search, filter, or favorite past results for quick reference.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips
* **For Research Papers:** Use the **Academic** domain with **Abstractive** summarization for better flow.
* **For Quick Scanning:** Use **Extractive** summarization with the **Tiny** length setting to find the "thesis" of a document instantly.
